# Constraint Optimization & Simulation Coupling

## Methodological Foundation
Patient waiting time and queue length in an emergency department are non-linear, stochastic phenomena generated by discrete-event queues with bursty arrivals and multi-resource contention. They cannot be optimized purely via simple linear programming constraints without gross distortion.

MedFlow solves this through a hybrid optimization architecture:
1. **Google OR-Tools CP-SAT:** Formulates and solves a multi-shift constraint integer program ensuring all clinical safety ratios, budget limits, and minimum coverage levels are strictly guaranteed.
2. **Simulation-Based Validation with Common Random Numbers (CRN):** Candidate staffing allocations generated by CP-SAT are executed through identical stochastic SimPy seeds to estimate true waiting time reductions, P95 tail improvements, and throughput gains without random comparison noise.

## Mathematical Formulation (CP-SAT Model)

### Decision Variables
- \(x_{m, \text{md}}, x_{e, \text{md}}, x_{n, \text{md}} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\): Physicians per shift (Morning, Evening, Night).
- \(x_{m, \text{rn}}, x_{e, \text{rn}}, x_{n, \text{rn}} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\): Nurses per shift (Morning, Evening, Night).
- \(x_{\text{diag}} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\): Diagnostic imaging stations.
- \(x_{\text{bed}} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\): Additional operational treatment beds.

### Constraints
1. **Budget Ceiling Constraint:**
   \[
   8 \cdot c_{\text{md}} \sum_{s} x_{s, \text{md}} + 8 \cdot c_{\text{rn}} \sum_{s} x_{s, \text{rn}} + 24 \cdot c_{\text{diag}} x_{\text{diag}} + 24 \cdot c_{\text{bed}} (\text{Beds}_0 + x_{\text{bed}}) \le \text{Budget}_{\text{total}}
   \]
2. **Clinical Safety Ratio (Nurse-to-Physician Ratio):**
   \[
   x_{s, \text{rn}} \ge 1.6 \cdot x_{s, \text{md}} \quad \forall s \in \{\text{Morning}, \text{Evening}, \text{Night}\}
   \]
   Guarantees adequate nursing staff for triage, bedside monitoring, and medication administration.
3. **Diurnal Peak Coverage:**
   \[
   x_{\text{morning}, \text{rn}} \ge \text{RN}_{\text{min, peak}}, \quad x_{\text{evening}, \text{rn}} \ge \text{RN}_{\text{min, peak}}
   \]
4. **Physical Capacity Bounds:**
   \[
   x_{\text{diag}} \in [2, 8], \quad x_{\text{bed}} \in [0, 15]
   \]

### Objective Function
Maximize clinical coverage score during historical peak arrival hours subject to the budget limit:
\[
\max \sum_{s} w_s \cdot (3 x_{s, \text{md}} + 2 x_{s, \text{rn}}) + 150 \cdot x_{\text{diag}} + 80 \cdot x_{\text{bed}}
\]
where \(w_{\text{morning}} = 40, w_{\text{evening}} = 40, w_{\text{night}} = 20\).
